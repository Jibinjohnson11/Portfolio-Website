Yes. Before a redesign I map every old URL to its new one, set up 301 redirects, keep the content that already ranks and check indexing after launch, so you keep the traffic you have already earned.

When someone in Thiruvananthapuram searches for “dentist near me”, “PSC coaching in Kesavadasapuram” or “homestay in Varkala”, the first thing they usually see is not a website — it's the map with three business listings underneath. Those listings come from **Google Business Profile**, and for most local businesses they bring in more calls than the website itself.

This guide walks through exactly how I optimise a profile for Trivandrum businesses.

### Why your Google Business Profile matters

Google decides which businesses appear in the map results mainly on three things: **relevance** (does your profile match the search?), **distance** (how close are you to the searcher?) and **prominence** (how well known and well reviewed are you?). You can't move your shop closer to every customer, but you can make your profile far more relevant and prominent than your competitors'.

### 1. Get the basics exactly right

- **Business name:** use your real-world name exactly as it appears on your signboard. Adding keywords like “Best Dental Clinic Trivandrum” to your name breaks Google's guidelines and can get the profile suspended.
- **Address or service area:** if customers visit you, show your address. If you travel to customers (plumbers, tutors, photographers), set a service area and hide the address.
- **Phone and website:** use a number you actually answer, and link to the most relevant page on your site.
- **Opening hours:** keep them accurate and add special hours for Onam, Vishu, Christmas and other holidays, so people don't arrive to a closed shutter.

### 2. Choose the right categories

Your **primary category** is one of the strongest signals for map rankings. Pick the most specific option that describes your main business — “Dermatologist” rather than just “Doctor”, “Coaching center” rather than “School”. Then add a few secondary categories for other services you genuinely offer. Look at the top three competitors for your main search to see which categories they use.

### 3. Add services and a clear description

List each service separately with a short explanation, using the words customers actually search for. Write a description that says what you do, who you serve and which areas of Trivandrum you cover — naturally, without stuffing keywords.

### 4. Upload real photos, regularly

Add your exterior (so people recognise the building from the road), interior, team, products and work samples. Real photos taken on your premises build far more trust than stock images. Add a few new ones every month to show the business is active.

### 5. Build a steady flow of genuine reviews

Reviews influence both your ranking and whether people choose you. The most reliable method is simple: ask every happy customer, at the moment they're happiest.

- Copy your review link from the profile and send it on WhatsApp right after a successful visit or delivery.
- Reply to every review — thank positive ones and respond calmly and helpfully to negative ones.
- Never buy reviews or offer discounts in exchange for them. It breaks Google's policies and fake reviews can be removed in bulk.

### 6. Publish posts

Posts let you share offers, events, new services and updates directly on your profile. A short post every week or two — a new batch starting, a festival offer, a recent project — keeps your listing fresh and gives searchers another reason to contact you.

### 7. Q&A is gone — answer questions on your website instead

Google has phased out the old Questions & Answers section on Business Profiles. In India, it had already disappeared from many profiles back in 2024. In its place, Google is rolling out AI-powered answers that draw on your profile, your reviews and your website.

**What this means for you:** the questions customers used to ask on your listing should now be answered clearly on your website — on service pages and in an FAQ section — and in your profile's services and description. The more complete and accurate that information is, the better Google's AI can answer on your behalf.

### 8. Keep your details consistent everywhere

Your business name, address and phone number (often called NAP) should be identical on your website, Google Business Profile, Justdial, Sulekha, IndiaMART, Facebook and any industry directories. Inconsistent details make Google less confident about your business.

- Duplicate profiles for the same business, often created by previous staff or agencies
- Old phone numbers that nobody answers any more
- No photos, or only a logo
- Reviews left unanswered for months
- Website link pointing to a slow homepage instead of a relevant, fast page

## What Is a RAG Chatbot? A Plain-English Guide for Kerala Businesses

By Jibin Johnson · 6 November 2026 · 3 min read

**In this article**

1. [The problem with ordinary chatbots](#problem)
2. [How RAG works](#how)

Every business has the same questions arriving again and again: *What are your timings? What's the fee? Do you have parking? Is the next batch full?* AI chatbots promise to handle these automatically — but many give vague or simply wrong answers. A **RAG chatbot** fixes that. Here's what it is, in plain English.

### The problem with ordinary chatbots

There are two common kinds of chatbot. **Scripted bots** follow fixed menus (“Press 1 for fees”). They're reliable but frustrating, because customers rarely ask questions the way the script expects. **Generic AI chatbots** understand natural language, but they answer from general knowledge learned from the internet — not from your business. Ask one about your fees and it may confidently invent a number.

### How RAG works

RAG stands for **Retrieval-Augmented Generation**. Think of it as an open-book exam instead of a memory test:

1. **Retrieve:** when a customer asks a question, the system first searches your own content — website pages, PDFs, price lists, policies — and finds the most relevant passages.
2. **Augment:** those passages are handed to the AI model along with the question.
3. **Generate:** the AI writes a natural answer based on what it was given, and can be told to say “I don't know” when the answer isn't there.

The result is a chatbot that talks like a person but answers like someone who has read your documents carefully.

### Examples for Kerala businesses

- **Coaching centre:** answers questions about courses, eligibility, batch timings and fees from the prospectus, and collects enquiries for admission.
- **Clinic:** explains services, doctor availability and preparation instructions — while never giving a diagnosis and always directing medical questions to a doctor.
- **Resort or homestay:** answers questions about rooms, check-in times, distance from the beach and airport transfers, any time of night.
- **Real estate developer:** shares project details, amenities and locations, and books site visits.
- **Internal use:** helps staff find answers in long SOPs and policy documents in seconds.

### What you need to get started

Less than most people expect. The essential ingredient is good content: your website, brochures, price lists, FAQs and policies. If that information is accurate and up to date, the chatbot will be too. You'll also want to decide where the chatbot lives (usually your website) and where leads should go — WhatsApp, email or a CRM.

### What it costs

There are two parts. The **build** covers preparing your content, setting up the retrieval system, writing guardrails, testing and adding the chat widget to your site. The **running cost** depends mainly on how many conversations the bot handles, because AI models are usually billed by usage. For most small and medium businesses, the monthly cost is modest compared with the staff time it saves — but it's worth getting an estimate for both parts before you start.

### Honest limitations

- It is only as good as your content. Outdated documents produce outdated answers.
- It reduces made-up answers dramatically, but no AI is perfect — testing and guardrails matter.
- It should hand over to a human for complaints, complex cases and anything sensitive.

### Is a RAG chatbot right for your business?

It's a strong fit if you answer the same questions daily, get enquiries outside working hours, or have information spread across many documents. It's probably not worth it yet if you receive only a handful of enquiries a week.

## Why Your Website Isn't Showing on Google (and How to Fix It)

By Jibin Johnson · 6 November 2026 · 3 min read

**In this article**

1. [First: is your site indexed?](#check)
2. [9 common reasons](#reasons)
3. [Quick fix checklist](#fix)

You've paid for a website, it looks great — and when you search for your business on Google, it's nowhere to be found. This is one of the most common problems I see with Kerala businesses. The good news: the cause is usually one of a handful of fixable issues.

### First: check whether Google has indexed your site

Type `site:yourdomain.com` into Google. If no results appear, Google hasn't indexed your site at all — that's a technical problem. If pages do appear but not for the searches you care about, the site is indexed but not ranking — that's usually a content, competition or authority problem.

For a precise answer, add your site to **Google Search Console** (it's free) and use the URL Inspection tool on your key pages. It tells you exactly whether each page is indexed and, if not, why.

### 9 common reasons your website isn't showing

#### 1. The website is brand new

New sites can take days or weeks to be discovered and longer to rank. Submitting a sitemap in Search Console speeds up discovery.

#### 2. A “noindex” tag or robots.txt is blocking Google

Developers often block search engines while a site is being built — and forget to remove the block at launch. Check for a `noindex` meta tag, the “discourage search engines” setting in WordPress, and your `robots.txt` file.

#### 3. No sitemap or Search Console setup

Without a sitemap, Google has to find your pages on its own. A submitted XML sitemap tells it exactly which pages exist.

#### 4. You're targeting the wrong keywords

Ranking for “web design” across India is very hard. Ranking for “web designer in Kottayam” or “bridal makeup in Thrissur” is far more realistic — and those searchers are closer to becoming customers.

#### 5. Thin or duplicate content

A page with two lines of text, or copied from another site, gives Google little reason to rank it. Each important page needs genuinely useful content that answers what searchers want to know.

#### 6. Content that only appears after JavaScript runs

Some websites built as single-page apps send Google an almost empty page and load the text with JavaScript. Google can process JavaScript, but it's slower and less reliable. Important content should be in the HTML from the start.

#### 7. The site is slow or hard to use on mobile

Most searches in Kerala happen on phones. Heavy images, bloated themes and too many plugins slow pages down, which hurts both rankings and enquiries.

#### 8. No local signals

For local searches, Google relies heavily on your [Google Business Profile](/blog/google-business-profile-trivandrum/), consistent business listings and reviews. Without them, it's hard to appear for “near me” searches.

#### 9. No authority yet

Google trusts sites that other reputable sites mention and link to. Local directories, industry listings, partners and press coverage all help build that authority over time.

### Quick fix checklist

- Add the site to Google Search Console and submit your sitemap
- Remove any accidental `noindex` tags or robots.txt blocks
- Give every page a unique title, description and H1 that match a realistic search
- Expand thin pages with genuinely helpful content
- Make sure the main content is in the HTML, not loaded later by JavaScript
- Compress images and test speed with PageSpeed Insights
- Set up and complete your Google Business Profile
- List your business consistently on trusted directories
